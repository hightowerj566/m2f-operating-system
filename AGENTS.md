- Keep the first-login profile, optional community invitation, and final ready screen in the same onboarding flow; this preserves setup progress until the member explicitly enters the app.
- Access resolves server-side in check-subscription as coach/admin → active 1:1 coaching (profiles.coaching_status) → Stripe; DB triggers block new check-ins/responses/priorities unless coaching is active. Why: invite-based coaching grants access without Stripe and must not be self-grantable.
- Members without a subscription land in the app (Home/More/Progress open); the pricing view never renders first. Locked tabs open the in-app upgrade modal instead. Why: subscription is asked for when a member touches a locked feature, not at login.
- The / route waits for auth restoration, shows signed-in members the dashboard, redirects recognized signed-out devices to /auth, and otherwise shows the public landing page; the Readiness Score stays public. Why: returning members skip marketing without bypassing authentication.
- Store device recognition as a boolean-only localStorage hint through memberDevice; never store identity or use the hint for authorization, and allow clearing it on the login screen. Why: navigation convenience must remain device-local and independent of session persistence.
